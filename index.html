Greetings!

I build software and study the data behind it. I'm a computer science student at De La Salle University, Manila, majoring in Software Technology and minoring in Data Science, and a DOST-SEI Scholar. My research asks how AI can support learning and make its estimates easier to inspect.

Teaching machines to teach

My thesis work is CLive, part of AnimoRank, an automated assessment platform for DLSU's introductory C programming course. CLive scaffolds students through a problem-solving framework instead of handing them answers, and the question I care about is behavioral: does structured guidance change how a novice approaches a problem? Prototype testing is underway, and the experimental study runs this November. The projects page has more.

Artificially replicating human intelligence

I'm interested in AI research, especially in how machines estimate what they see and in making those estimates inspectable. With classmates, I've worked on apparent age estimation using the IMDB-WIKI dataset and the DEX method, and on Ceci, an experimental empathic AI-enhanced IDE that we evaluated against VS Code and ChatGPT. Both are covered on the publications page.

Making things that make things convenient

I'm also a software engineer, and I'm drawn to tools that make everyday work easier. My coursework has given me a chance to build real solutions, from a video game review site to a database for a music collection.

Working hard, playing hard

When I'm not working, I'm probably asleep or playing retro games. The simplicity of older titles, like the original Super Mario Bros., made me wonder whether I could make something like them. That question led to a level editor for a game I was working on, and it was the first time I noticed my own idea of making things more convenient. I also watch slice-of-life anime and manga, like K-ON and Bocchi the Rock!
